Projects

Selected work

Corporate governance · Financial statement analysis Corporate Internship Project · Apr – Jul 2026

Corporate governance quality and financial performance: Coal India Limited, FY2020–21 to FY2024–25

Objective

To examine how governance quality and financial performance moved together at Coal India Limited across five financial years.

Tools and methods

Annual report and Corporate Governance Report review, a seven-dimension scoring model, Excel ratio analysis, three-stage DuPont decomposition and Pearson correlation.

My contribution

  • Constructed a Governance Quality Index (GQI) on a 105-point scale.
  • Calculated Net Profit Margin, ROCE, EBITDA Margin and ROE.
  • Analysed ROE through margin, asset turnover and leverage.
  • Reported correlations as descriptive associations only.
Scope: single company, five annual observations (n = 5). Results are descriptive; correlation does not establish causation, and no statistical significance is claimed. Advanced time-series tests were deliberately not applied because of the small sample.

DuPont view of ROE

ROE = Net Profit Margin × Asset Turnover × Financial Leverage. Margins expanded and leverage fell while asset turnover stayed between 0.55× and 0.62×. ROE peaked at 46.31% in FY2022–23 and eased to 35.68% by FY2024–25.
